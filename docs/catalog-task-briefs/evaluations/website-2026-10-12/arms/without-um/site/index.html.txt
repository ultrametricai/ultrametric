        <div class="faq-intro"><p class="eyebrow section-kicker">03 / A FEW MORE DETAILS</p><h2 id="faq-title">Glad you<br><em>asked.</em></h2><p>Here’s where things stand<br>and where we’re headed.</p><div class="faq-note"><span class="drawn-star">✳</span> Early days.<br>Thoughtful next steps.</div></div>
        <div class="faq-list">
          <details open><summary>Who is Fieldnote Work for?<svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#plus"/></svg></summary><p>Independent property managers with portfolios of roughly 5–30 buildings, in the United States and Canada. We’re interested in how your team handles maintenance requests today.</p></details>
          <details><summary>Can I start using Fieldnote Work today?<svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#plus"/></svg></summary><p>Fieldnote Work is an early prototype, not a generally available service. The current prototype turns form entries into work-order drafts for human review. Right now, we’re seeking discovery conversations to understand what property managers need.</p></details>
          <details><summary>Can I forward maintenance emails into it?<svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#plus"/></svg></summary><p>Email import is unfinished. The current prototype starts with a form entry. If email is part of your workflow, we’d like to hear how you handle those requests.</p></details>
          <details><summary>Does it send work orders automatically?<svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#plus"/></svg></summary><p>The current prototype creates drafts for human review. You review the details and decide on the next step. The example on this website doesn’t send, submit or save a work order.</p></details>
          <details><summary>What would a discovery conversation cover?<svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#plus"/></svg></summary><p>How requests arrive, the details you need before taking action, and what makes a useful work-order draft. It’s a chance to share your experience and help shape the prototype. There’s no paid checkout or subscription to start here.</p></details>
