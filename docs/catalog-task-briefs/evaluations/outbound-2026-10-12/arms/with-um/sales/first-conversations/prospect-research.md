## Questions that can disqualify a candidate quickly

- How many buildings does the manager actually oversee? Do not substitute units, listings, client portfolios, or historical transactions for building count.
- Do their own staff submit maintenance notes for manager review, or are requests entirely tenant-to-vendor?
- Does the existing system already turn those notes into usable work orders with little effort?
- Would a separate form create more work than it removes? Is email ingestion required before the prototype would be useful?

The working hypothesis is that gathering missing information or rewriting notes may be a repeated task. This is an inference to test, not a fact about any listed organization. Existing request forms are a reason to explore the next step in the workflow, not evidence of dissatisfaction.

## Boundaries of this shortlist

All four remain held in the ledger. Public access authorized research; it did not establish permission to send. No tenant request form or emergency maintenance line should be used for a sales approach. No personal email addresses were guessed, no email delivery was tested, and no automated enrichment was run.

Ellsworth is first because its stated building count matches. Rany is second because it identifies an internal maintenance team. AMP and Apartment Solutions are fallback research candidates with weaker size evidence. No Canadian recipients are included in this first batch.
