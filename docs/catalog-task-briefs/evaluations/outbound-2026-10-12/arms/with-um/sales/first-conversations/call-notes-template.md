# 20-minute first conversation

Purpose: discover whether staff-note intake is a repeated, important problem and whether a manager-reviewed draft would help. Use the prospect's account; do not assume the problem exists. Discuss a redacted or invented example rather than collecting tenant details.

| Minutes | Prompts |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3 | What is your role? How many buildings do you oversee? Who reports maintenance issues and who prepares the work order? |
| 3–9 | Walk me through the most recent issue your own staff reported. Where did the note arrive? What happened next? What information was missing? Did anyone rewrite it? |
| 9–13 | How often does that happen? What effort or delay did this particular example involve? What system do you use now, and what works well? Record their estimate as an estimate. |
| 13–17 | Fieldnote currently takes a staff form entry and creates a draft for manager review. Email import is unfinished. Would that fit any part of the process you described? What would make a separate form impractical? |
| 17–20 | Who would decide to try or buy something for this step? What would they need to see? If there is a fit, agree on one next step: a prototype walkthrough using an invented example. If there is no fit, record why and close. |

Do not describe unverified integrations, automated dispatch, savings, customers, or security commitments. No price or pilot terms are established; capture pricing questions for the founder rather than making a commitment.

- Missing information / rewriting / impact, in their words:
- Frequency and effort estimate, clearly attributed:
- What currently works well:
- Form-entry fit / email-import requirement:
- Decision-maker, other stakeholders, purchasing process:
- Budget and timing: unknown unless explicitly discussed
- Exact agreed next step, owner and date:
- Founder interpretation (separate from the facts above):
- Disposition: learning only / potential fit / qualified follow-up / no fit

Only record a qualified follow-up after an attended call establishes relevant scope, a concrete workflow need, a plausible fit for current capabilities, the decision process, and a mutually agreed next step. If staff already create usable orders directly or email import is essential, record the mismatch. A request for information, polite interest or offered time alone is not qualification.

After the first three attended calls, compare the repeated facts. Decide whether to continue with this audience, narrow to a more specific workflow, or change the offer. Preserve negative evidence and do not turn these calls into testimonials without explicit permission.
