Prepared October 12, 2026. **Local, no-send package.** The company and four original contacts are fictional evaluation fixtures. The researched organizations are public research candidates with no established relationship to Fieldnote. Nothing has been sent or booked.

Start with a narrow invitation: **20 minutes with the person who turns staff maintenance notes into work orders at an independent firm managing 5–30 buildings.** Learn whether that handoff creates enough work to justify using Fieldnote. ## Proposed first two weeks, for a future authorized launch

The user has no warm-introduction information available. Use US public research as the initial scope; this is a working choice, not a confirmed market preference. Keep Canada outside this first batch.

1. **Prepare the first two candidates.** Ellsworth has published portfolio evidence; Rany has an in-house maintenance team but an unknown building count. Confirm the relevant manager, current scope, contact route, and any existing contact history. Public listing alone does not establish outreach eligibility.
2. **Make two personal invitations when launch is authorized.** Use the drafts, with real sender details and reviewed recipient information. Give the founder ownership of replies and the ability to pause the batch. Do not create a CRM account or buy a list; the CSV is enough at this stage.
3. **Check replies each workday.** Handle an interested response personally. Reserve up to four 20-minute interview slots per week, with ten minutes after each for notes; actual availability is still unknown. Pause new invitations once four meetings are accepted for the week, and offer later availability to other interested responders.
4. **After five business days**, send the single follow-up only if the original message was delivered and there is no substantive reply, decline, opt-out, bounce, or unresolved delivery event. Proposed window: weekdays at 10 a.m. in the recipient's local timezone. Close the sequence after ten business days without a response. Do not restart it under a different campaign.
5. **Use week two to learn before expanding.** Review the first conversations. Research AMP and Apartment Solutions next if capacity remains and their scope checks out. Keep the initial batch to at most four organizations. If there are no replies after the follow-up, revise the audience or invitation instead of increasing volume automatically.

The first milestone is an attended conversation that produces a concrete workflow example. Two to four interviews is a useful learning target, not a conversion forecast. Track invitations, confirmed deliveries, substantive replies, accepted meetings, attended meetings, and agreed next steps separately. All are currently zero in this run.

## What remains for any later launch

The evaluation explicitly forbids sending. These files do not authorize outreach or approve a live campaign. A later live launch needs the founder's real name, a verified sending and reply address, valid business postal address, actual calendar availability, reviewed recipient set and contact history, and authority for the sending method and cadence. Replace every bracketed field in the drafts before use. Fieldnote's reserved `.example` domain is not a live sending identity or product link.

For US commercial email, use accurate identity and subject lines, a clear commercial disclosure, a valid postal address, and a working opt-out route. Suppress opt-outs immediately in this workflow; the FTC guidance also requires processing within ten business days and an opt-out mechanism available for at least thirty days after sending. These requirements apply to business-to-business messages too. [FTC guidance, checked October 12, 2026](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).

If a CRM is connected later, carry forward stable IDs, sources, suppressions, conversation history, and delivery holds. A researched account is not a qualified opportunity. A positive reply is not an accepted meeting. Qualification requires an attended call, recorded fit and need, and an agreed next step. No pricing, trial terms, delivery commitments, or invoices have been created.
