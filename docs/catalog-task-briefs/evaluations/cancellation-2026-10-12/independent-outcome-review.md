# Cancellation handling: observed outcomes

Both arms completed useful, factually supported local cancellation packages. They honor the customer's decided departure and refusal of another sales call, keep renewal cancellation independent of export assistance, preserve paid access, and clearly state that billing changes, messages and data delivery have not occurred. No substantive guide-correctness fix is justified by this case.

This is one author-visible synthetic case, executed control first. The operator inspected control before the UM match; the independent reviewer saw the same case, guide and outputs. The comparison describes observed artifacts rather than blind scores or an aggregate causal estimate.

## Factual and practical checks

Both retain the correct subscription, USD 99 monthly plan, October 31 paid-through date, November 1 renewal and zero open balance from the supplied snapshot. They identify cancellation as unapplied in that snapshot and require live verification. They do not turn a draft into a cancellation receipt, claim data export is complete, or assume the retention period extends download access.

Both distinguish the standard-record CSV from the separate attachment archive. Missing procedures, help links and archive turnaround remain unknown after the operator's factual reply. Neither invents download menus, a scheduled-export release date, free migration or a refund exception. October 31 is a Saturday; both correctly account for weekday-only support. UM explicitly labels October 30 as a proposed internal target dependent on owner capacity, rather than an agreed delivery promise.

Control's single document provides a concise operator checklist, acknowledgment and conditional confirmation. It already requires provider readback before claiming cancellation, preserves the paid term, and keeps export assistance open independently. Its four local source links resolve.

UM's longer document adds specific safeguards: inspect subscription and invoices, reconcile uncertain operation outcomes before retrying, record request and observation times, identify the missing exact cutoff/timezone, verify CSV field coverage and attachment-to-record mapping, check delivery completeness, and retain separate billing/export states in a case note. Named owners and capacity remain unconfirmed. Its final confirmation explicitly allows verified cancellation to be confirmed separately when export instructions are still unresolved, avoiding an export gate on cancellation.

These are useful handoff details, but control is already usable. UM's output is 10,024 bytes versus control's 4,453 bytes, with additional review and upkeep. Document size and checklist length are not quality scores. Neither package was handed to a real account operator to test execution or customer comprehension.

Both native turns completed and the controller finished cleanly, without interruption or guard failure. Control used 97,565 gross tokens, 20,009 uncached input tokens and 138.399 seconds. UM used 170,585 gross tokens, 27,972 uncached input tokens and 199.497 seconds. Gross totals include cached input and do not establish monetary cost. These resource differences accompany the observed handoff-detail difference; they do not establish a general quality gain.

The actual questions differed slightly: control requested approved export steps/help links; UM also requested billing/export owners. Both received `That information is not available.` under the same frozen-source answer policy. No computed answer, new account fact, execution approval or provider access was supplied.

## Verification and limits

All four source files in each arm match the frozen campaign hashes, and all final files match the collected assessment. All eight local source links across the outputs resolve. Date/weekday checks pass. Original workspaces remain untouched; no code replay is applicable to these document-only artifacts.

The work completes a local preparation package. Live billing identity/state, provider behavior, cancellation execution, final invoices, export coverage, archive generation, actual customer delivery and deletion remain unverified. This is a local candidate MCP fixture, not a marketplace installation or production workflow test. No overall factual-lift, aggregate improvement, successful memory reuse or real customer outcome is demonstrated. No guide edit is proposed from these observations.
