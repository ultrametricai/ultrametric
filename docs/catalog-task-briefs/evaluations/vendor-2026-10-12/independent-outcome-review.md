## Evidence, cost and exit-path gaps

Control's proposed monthly base totals are arithmetically consistent: Zoho Enterprise USD 150 for three seats, Professional USD 105, Attio Plus USD 132 and Pro USD 297. Its Pipedrive USD 1,404 annual total remains explicitly an annual offer, with monthly billing unverified. Tax, add-ons, implementation effort and future assistant cost are excluded, with a complete order total still required before purchase. The recommendation is pilot-only; it does not establish a final affordable configuration.

One source-freshness limitation remains: the [Zoho comparison page](https://www.zoho.com/crm/comparison.html?src=buy_crm) shows the stated monthly prices but labels its update date June 4, 2022. A checked-on date is not proof of a fresh commercial offer. This does not establish that the quoted number is wrong; it reinforces the need for current plan and total-order confirmation. [Attio pricing](https://attio.com/pricing) supports the cited base amounts, and its [custom-object documentation](https://attio.com/help/reference/managing-your-data/objects/create-and-manage-custom-objects) supports the Pro/Enterprise restriction.

Control treats permanent locations as an explicit proposed model, with an unresolved owner choice. It does not claim a deal-level location field is equivalent to a reusable location/contact relationship. It flags conflicting Zoho import documentation: the [module importer](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/import-data/articles/import-data-into-a-crm-module?crmprod=1) supports dependent association files, while the [linking-module page](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-modules/articles/linking-modules) still reports an import limitation. Those documents justify a configured-account test, not an assumed pass or failure.

Neither arm proves that the company can reconstruct relationships, notes and quote bytes from an export. Control correctly separates file bytes from login-dependent links and a TXT fixture from PDF recovery. It retains Zoho's [backup exclusions](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/data-backup/articles/requesting-data-backup). UM commentary's Capsule warning is supported by [Capsule's export documentation](https://capsulecrm.com/support/account-and-security/exporting-and-canceling-your-account/), which excludes attachments from full export. No reviewer accessed a live CRM account or tested those product operations.

No intended assistant was connected to a CRM, and documented APIs/scopes are not receipts for permitted updates, denied access, retries or revocation. Both preserve the boundary between research and a real purchase, account grant, data upload, agreement or rollout. Actual usability and switching effort remain unresolved. No full-lifecycle cost or purchase-ready winner was delivered.

## Observed process issue and scoped correction

The concrete issue is loss of durable usefulness under a research bound: UM made 43 completed native web-search items and reached the cap with no saved artifact. Control made 23 such items and retained partial work. These are native action counts, not billed calls or quality scores. Both also asked about seats, budget and tools already recorded in the input packet; questions about an absent sales trigger or unresolved schema choice are distinct and remain legitimate gaps.

A small guide correction is grounded in this observation: save the buying brief and a compact requirement matrix with unknown entries before broad market research; then update source, plan, date and remaining checks as research proceeds. Bound repeated searches that no longer resolve the decision and preserve a provisional or trial-only outcome when necessary. Missing price, relationship or export evidence must remain unknown.

The later working-tree change to `vendor_011.n1.guidance` and `n2.guidance` implements that sequence. Independent static review found no blocking contradiction, added external-action authority, new approval gate or graph change. It is more concrete than the earlier instruction to record priorities and retain comparison rows later. The trials in this bundle used immutable revision `110ff2631ac0adf37f5325c123391af643a4a7c5`; the later wording has **not** been performer-retested. See [guide-change review](guide-change-review.json). No claim that this change improves completion or quality is supported yet.
