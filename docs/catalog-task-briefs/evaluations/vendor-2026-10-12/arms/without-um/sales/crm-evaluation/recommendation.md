**CRM recommendation for Rivet Desk — 12 October 2026**

Trial **Zoho CRM Enterprise first**, on monthly billing: USD 50 per user × 3 = **USD 150/month before tax**. This leaves USD 30 against the stated ceiling. It is the strongest documented fit among the three vendors examined for an inbox plus persistent organization/location/contact relationships. This is a recommendation for a pilot, not a claim that a vendor has passed testing. [Zoho pricing](https://www.zoho.com/crm/comparison.html?src=buy_crm)

Your confirmation establishes three seats, Google Workspace, spreadsheets, PDF quotes, a USD 180 monthly ceiling, monthly billing preferred, and no immediate automation requirement. The local workflow adds a critical relationship requirement: one firm owns several locations, and one contact can serve several locations. Pending clarification, this recommendation assumes locations need lasting records, including after a deal closes.

The local company and pipeline files explicitly contain synthetic examples. The company record describes 310 organizations, but the supplied CSV contains only three opportunity rows. This review does not treat that sample as the full pipeline or as real customer evidence. **Why Enterprise earns the first pilot.** Zoho documents multi-select lookup fields in Enterprise and above; these represent many-to-many relationships. It also provides a dedicated SalesInbox in Enterprise. Proposed configuration: Accounts for property firms; a Locations module with an Account lookup; Contacts linked to Locations through a many-to-many relationship; Deals linked to the appropriate Account, Location and Contact. Keep one contact record per person. Store the sales owner on the deal and the follow-up owner/due date on its task. [Feature limits](https://www.zoho.com/crm/complete-feature-list.html), [relationship model](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-modules/articles/linking-modules), [SalesInbox setup](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm-v2-1/salesinbox/setting-up-salesinbox/articles/configure-salesinbox-for-zoho-crm)

Professional deserves a look only if reducing cost is more important than the dedicated inbox and native multi-select relationship editor. The current detailed feature table lists custom modules and five lookup fields per module for Professional, suggesting an explicit membership module with Contact and Location lookups. Another Zoho comparison page has older, conflicting custom-module limits. Validate the target account's capabilities before relying on this cheaper design. [Current detailed table](https://www.zoho.com/crm/complete-feature-list.html), [conflicting comparison](https://www.zoho.com/crm/comparison.html?src=buy_crm)

**Why the alternatives are conditional.** Pipedrive Growth includes Gmail synchronization. Its native person record links to one organization; one person can have several deals. A firm plus one deal per location could suit a simple pipeline, but does not establish independent location/contact relationships. That compromise needs explicit acceptance. [Growth features](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/pricing), [Pipedrive data model](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/how-is-pipedrive-data-organized)

Attio's limitation is plan access, not a general inability to model relationships: Pro supports the required custom structure, at a price above this budget. [Custom objects](https://attio.com/help/reference/managing-your-data/objects/create-and-manage-custom-objects), [relationship attributes](https://attio.com/help/reference/managing-your-data/attributes/relationship-attributes)

**Import and exit need proof.** Zoho's newer importer documents importing dependent association files, including multi-select relationships. Its relationship article still says those fields are unsupported during import. Use the new importer in the pilot and confirm what the account actually exposes. File attachments require a separate migration/upload path; a CSV containing a quote path does not upload the quote. [New importer](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/import-data/articles/import-data-into-a-crm-module?crmprod=1), [older limitation](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/customize-crm-account/customizing-modules/articles/linking-modules)

Zoho documents separate data and attachment backup archives. Email content, email attachments and Documents are excluded from that backup. Save each quote as a CRM record attachment as well as keeping the original, then prove it can be downloaded and matched to the correct deal. Verify notes, association rows and stable IDs in the actual export; a backup feature is not proof of a complete migration. [Backup documentation](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/data-administration/data-backup/articles/requesting-data-backup)

Pipedrive exports activities, notes and files separately; Google Drive files are excluded from its global export. Attio's workspace export lists notes and files as CSV data, but that alone does not establish recovery of quote file bytes; emails are excluded. Neither alternative has a verified exit path in this assessment. [Pipedrive exports](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/exporting-data-from-pipedrive), [Attio backups](https://attio.com/help/reference/imports-exports/back-up-your-workspace-data)

**A small pilot that can settle the choice.** Use [the prepared fixture](pilot-fixture.json) and [the checklist](pilot-checklist.csv). These describe a test; they are not vendor import receipts or a ready-to-upload vendor template. Adapt field mapping and required fields in the target CRM.

1. Import two firms, three locations, two contacts, three contact/location links and three deals. Contact P-01 must remain one record shared by Cedar's North and West locations. Keep the two deals' stages, owners and last-touch dates separate.
2. Have each teammate find the right contact/history, assign a follow-up and move a deal. Suggested acceptance target: each does this in under two minutes without help after initial orientation. Test Gmail visibility using controlled messages and confirm shared-contact emails are associated with the right deal.
3. Add a clearly marked test note and quote attachment to Cedar West. Export and reconstruct the relationships locally. Match the note, owner, dates, quote bytes and attachment-to-deal link. The supplied quote is a TXT draft, not a PDF; an actual representative PDF still needs testing.
4. Choose Enterprise only if the relationship, inbox, ownership and exit checks pass and the complete monthly cost fits USD 180. If location records prove unnecessary, test Pipedrive Growth next after confirming monthly pricing. If Zoho's configuration is burdensome, reassess the model before buying a year of any CRM.

**Future assistant access remains unproven.** The founder note asks for scoped reading and updating. Zoho documents module-specific OAuth READ and UPDATE scopes, but this does not prove compatibility with the intended assistant. Identify that assistant, verify its actual available tools, then test reading a pilot record, updating a permitted field, reading it back, denied operations, and access revocation. Confirm any extra seat or connector cost. No CRM connector was available in this session and no authenticated operation was attempted.
