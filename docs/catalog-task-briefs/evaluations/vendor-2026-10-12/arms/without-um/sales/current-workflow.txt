SYNTHETIC SALES OPERATIONS NOTES
The founder, one salesperson and the support lead all work from a shared spreadsheet. Email is Google Workspace. A weekly call goes row by row through opportunities. Quotes live as PDF files linked from the sheet. A property management firm can own several locations, and a contact may work for more than one location. Losing that relationship during import or export would be expensive. We need a usable inbox/activity history, follow-up ownership, organization/contact relationships, CSV import, and an exit path that includes notes and attached quotes. We have not trialed a CRM.
