# Lindy Documentation

- [What is Lindy?](https://docs.lindy.ai/index.md): Lindy is an AI employee. It lives in your Slack, connects to your tools, and comes back with the work done.
- [Quickstart](https://docs.lindy.ai/start-here/quickstart.md): Get Lindy into your team's Slack, then connect your own email, calendar, and phone. Two minutes.
- [Set up Lindy](https://docs.lindy.ai/teammate/setup.md): Get Lindy into your team's Slack, link everyone, and connect your email, calendar, and phone.
- [Use cases](https://docs.lindy.ai/use-cases.md): What teams actually ask Lindy for, by role. Scroll for ideas, then try the closest one on your own tools.
